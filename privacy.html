Privacy Policy for DreamList

Your dreams are personal. Everything stays on your device.

Last updated: [Date]

Overview

DreamList ("the app") is developed by David Anthold ("we"). The app is designed to work entirely on your device, and we do not collect your personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or receive any personal information or data you enter in the app. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we do not create user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Any content you create in the app, such as your dreams, notes, pictures, name, date of birth, and life expectancy goal, is stored only on your device. We cannot access it. If you back up your device, for example with iCloud or your computer, that content may be included in the backup. Backups are managed by Apple under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Your photos are your photos. You can add them from your library and attach to your dreams. Those pictures stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can change this permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Sharing of information

We do not sell, share, or transfer your information to third parties, because we do not collect it.

Purchases

The app is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store. Apple handles all payments and payment information. We do not receive your payment details.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data deletion

Because your data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting content in the app, uninstalling the app or doing a complete reset of all data and settings in the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at [URL], with the date updated above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to our support team.
