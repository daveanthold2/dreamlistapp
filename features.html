Features

Built to help you be intentional.

A bucket list is a collection of things to do. DreamList helps you capture why each one matters.

Your personal dream list

Add as many dreams as you like. Each one is numbered in the order you added it, and you can check it off when it comes true.

Categories that are yours

Create and customize your own categories so your list reflects what matters most to you, whether that's Adventure, Career, Health, Relationships, or something entirely your own.

Remember your why

Write down why each dream matters to you, with a live preview of your notes.

Add pictures

Attach photos to bring each dream to life.

Track your progress

See your total, active, and completed dreams, your completion rate, and a breakdown by category.

Make it personal

Add your name and birthday, and set a goal for how long you want to live, such as 90 years, to put your dreams in the context of your life.

Live with intention

Record the why behind every dream and focus on what truly matters to you.
